# Changelog

All notable changes to NPC Cards are documented here. This project adheres to
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.1.2] - 2026-10-31

### Changed

- Needs Personnel 2.4.4, the first version MLVScan 2.1.7 does not disable.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-10-31

### Changed

- New phone icon in the style of the game's own apps.
- The requirements now list S1API, which Personnel needs.

### Fixed

- Cards converted from another mod show their original author instead of the account that uploaded them. In the
  Installed tab this needs Personnel 2.4.3.
- The app now says "Personnel is not loaded." when Personnel is missing. Before, its icon did not show up at all.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-31

### Added

- The Browse tab recommends Siesta from 40 custom NPCs if it is not installed. Closing the tip lasts until you quit
  the game.

### Changed

- Needs Personnel 2.4.2, where big heads, tiny heads and missing heads show on NPCs from cards.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-30

### Changed
- New icon in the phone, the Side Hustle hub and on Thunderstore: an NPC card next to the NPC
  it brings into town.
- Needs Personnel 2.4.1, where NPCs from cards wear their shirts and trousers again.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- NPC Cards app on your phone, and with Side Hustle a main-menu gamemode: browse NPC Vault, filter by role, sort
  by new, hearts or downloads.
- Install a card from its detail page. The NPC moves into town after the next game start.
- The Installed tab lists every card you have, also those from Personify or the import folder, and removes them.
- Card PNGs in `UserData/Personnel/Import/` show up under Import and install with one click.
- Connect your NPC Vault account with a code to get cards you send with "Send to game" and to upload your own.
